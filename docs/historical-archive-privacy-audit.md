# Historical archive privacy audit

A selective historical brand package was reviewed before migration.

## Publication decision

Raw administrative, commercial, and office-document sources are **not** part of the public brand repository merely because they helped establish provenance.

The reviewed LibreCode provenance documents contain personal/fiscal identifiers, contact information, address-related content, and office-document author/editor metadata. Those source documents remain private historical records and are intentionally excluded from the public repository.

## What is retained publicly

Only:
- validated brand rules;
- current/historical design specifications needed to interpret the identity;
- sanitized provenance conclusions;
- approved artwork;
- licensing/trademark metadata.

## What remains private

- raw contracts and proposals;
- personal identifiers;
- private contact information;
- signatures and addresses;
- unrelated financial/commercial terms;
- office-document authorship metadata where it is not required for attribution;
- working files that do not add unique current brand information.

This audit follows the data-minimization policy in `docs/privacy-and-provenance.md`.
