# LibreCode editorial evidence

This document records official LibreCode sources used to complete descriptive and editorial content in the brand manual. The repository remains the canonical place where those facts are translated into brand guidance.

Reviewed on 2026-09-25.

## Official website

Source: https://librecode.coop/

The current LibreCode website supports the following descriptive statements:

- LibreCode presents itself as a digital cooperative of specialists in free-software development.
- Its public message connects technology with transparency and freedom.
- It describes cooperativism and software freedom through a shared idea of distributed power and visible decisions/code.
- Its public audience includes organizations in the public, private, and third sectors.
- Its offer includes implementation, hosting, support, customization, and operation of free-software solutions.
- It connects software freedom to control, autonomy, supplier choice, and the ability to adapt solutions.
- It explicitly treats community support as part of sustaining free software.

These facts do not authorize stronger claims such as absolute independence, guaranteed security, guaranteed sovereignty, or automatic elimination of operational risk. The repository's voice-and-tone rules remain controlling.

## Mission, vision, and values

Source: https://librecode.coop/jobs/pre-requisitos/

The official recruitment page currently states:

- mission: contribute to a solidarity economy through cooperativism, democratizing information technology, security, and data privacy with free software;
- vision: grow and foster new free-technology cooperatives, with the long-term objective of federation, mutual strengthening, visibility, and representation;
- values: solidarity economy, security and privacy, community, transparency, and copyleft/free code.

The brand manual may summarize these points, but should not silently rewrite or extend them into new institutional commitments.

## Cooperativism

Sources:

- https://librecode.coop/
- https://librecode.coop/posts/os-7-principios-do-cooperativismo
- https://librecode.coop/posts/cooperativismo-e-tecnologia-a-uniao-que-transforma

LibreCode publicly associates its cooperative identity with democratic management, economic participation, autonomy, education, intercooperation, and community interest. The brand manual should explain this relationship in plain language rather than treating "Coop" as a decorative suffix.

## Community

Source: https://librecode.coop/

The official website states that free software is sustained by community and invites support for fixes, features, maintenance, and auditable code. Brand communication may therefore describe community participation as part of the LibreCode ecosystem, while avoiding claims that every user, customer, contributor, or partner is a cooperative member.

## Boundaries

The following remain unresolved or require stronger evidence before becoming normative brand content:

- official INPI trademark process numbers, classes, and ownership metadata;
- any claim that a named third party is an official partner unless an authoritative current source proves that relationship;
- any new logo geometry, color, typography, slogan, certification, or endorsement not already approved in this repository;
- any claim of guaranteed security, compliance, sovereignty, or freedom from all dependency.
