# Privacy policy

This repository follows data minimization.

## Allowed

- current brand rules;
- canonical artwork;
- public brand contact channels;
- licensing and trademark information;
- research references needed by the brand system.

## Not allowed

- personal identifiers;
- private email addresses or phone numbers;
- private addresses;
- signatures;
- fiscal identifiers;
- contracts or proposals unrelated to the public brand contract;
- private commercial terms;
- unrelated document metadata.
