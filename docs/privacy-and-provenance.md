# Privacy and provenance policy

Historical design archives may contain personal data, commercial documents, contact details, identifiers, authorship metadata, and other material that is useful for provenance but inappropriate for a public brand repository.

## Public repository rule

Do **not** publish raw historical administrative or commercial documents merely to preserve design history.

Instead:

- extract only the brand rule, design rationale, ownership/provenance fact, or technical specification that is actually needed;
- rewrite that information in neutral documentation;
- omit personal identifiers, private contact details, signatures, addresses, fiscal identifiers, and unrelated commercial terms;
- strip author/editor metadata from public derivatives unless attribution is legally or historically necessary;
- do not publish source documents containing personal data when a sanitized factual note is sufficient;
- keep private historical originals in controlled archival storage.

## Artwork

Official artwork may be public when it is needed for legitimate brand use. Before publication, verify:

1. source/provenance;
2. copyright status;
3. trademark status;
4. whether editor metadata exposes personal information;
5. whether the file can be replaced by a cleaner canonical vector master.

## Fonts

Fonts retain their upstream licenses and copyright metadata. Do not relabel them as brand-owned content.

## Historical archive

The former Nextcloud folders remain a private provenance/archive layer after migration. Public share links should be revoked only after unique current assets have been reconciled with this repository.

## Data-minimization principle

The canonical repository should contain the **minimum information necessary to operate the brand system**, not a complete copy of the organization's historical files.
