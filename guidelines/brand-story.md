# Brand story

## Libre means freedom

LibreCode uses **Libre** in the free-software sense: freedom to understand, run, inspect, adapt, integrate, maintain, and evolve technology.

For customers and partners, that freedom should be communicated as practical autonomy:

- more control over technology and data;
- freedom to choose who operates or maintains a solution;
- ability to integrate with existing systems;
- reduced artificial dependency on a single supplier;
- continuity through documented, inspectable technology;
- the option to internalize knowledge or change providers.

The brand should avoid reducing “Libre” to “free of charge”. Price is a commercial term; freedom is the strategic value.
