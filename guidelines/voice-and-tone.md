# Voice and tone

LibreCode should sound technically competent, transparent, cooperative, and precise.

## Prefer
- concrete benefits over ideological slogans;
- verifiable claims over absolutes;
- “freedom to choose” over attacks on proprietary vendors;
- “reduced vendor lock-in” over “no dependency whatsoever”;
- plain language for business audiences while keeping technical accuracy.

## Avoid
- exaggerated security or sovereignty claims;
- implying that free software automatically eliminates operational risk;
- adversarial language toward customers who currently use proprietary systems;
- jargon when a clearer term exists.

The commercial narrative should connect software freedom to autonomy, continuity, interoperability, auditability, and choice.
