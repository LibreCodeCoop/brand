# Visual identity

## Canonical artwork

The canonical logo master is:

`source/artwork/librecode-logo.svg`

The current logo combines the connected-node symbol with the LibreCode wordmark.

## Approved colors

The logo uses:
- LibreCode Red: `#e5332a`;
- LibreCode Gray: `#7c7b7b`.

## Clear space

Maintain clear space around the mark sufficient to keep it visually independent from text, borders, and other marks.

The manual source contains the normative construction diagram. Do not recreate the logo by eye.

## Minimum size

The complete mark must remain legible. For print, do not use the complete logo below 20 mm width. For digital use, choose a size that preserves clean rendering and recognition on the target display.

## Rules

Do not:
- distort proportions;
- recolor the mark outside approved variants;
- rotate or skew the mark;
- add shadows, outlines, gradients, or effects;
- rearrange components;
- place the mark on a background that compromises contrast;
- create alternate “official” logo files outside the canonical source.

Derived PNG/PDF files are generated from the canonical SVG by CI.
