# Visual identity

The visual identity is a system, not a single logo file.

The completed specification must define:

- primary logo and symbol/logomark;
- horizontal/stacked variants when officially supported;
- monochrome and reversed variants;
- clear space;
- minimum digital and print size;
- approved and prohibited backgrounds;
- primary and supporting colors;
- typography and fallbacks;
- iconography/illustration treatment when applicable;
- examples of incorrect manipulation.

Canonical editable artwork belongs in `source/`. Approved exports belong in `assets/`.

Do not infer or invent a visual rule from an existing export. Rules must be verified against the current manual/source material during the migration audit.
