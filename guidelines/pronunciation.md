# Pronunciation

**Libre** begins with an /i/ sound, not the English diphthong in “lie”.

Reference IPA:

- **Libre**: /ˈli.bɾe/
- **LibreCode**: /ˈli.bɾe koʊd/

**Code** keeps its English pronunciation.

**Coop** is written without a hyphen. In speech, keep two syllables: “co” + “op”.

The final canonical IPA should be validated against an approved human recording before being treated as an immutable phonetic specification.
